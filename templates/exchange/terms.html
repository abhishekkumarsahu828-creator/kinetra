RESPONSIBLE SHARING

Usage principles.

Clear responsibilities for owners, managers and borrowers.

Access is conditional

Resource listings do not guarantee permission to use restricted equipment. An authorised institution determines eligibility, approval, supervision and physical access conditions.

Accurate handover records

Borrowers should document resource condition truthfully and follow the relevant safety and return procedures. Owners or authorised staff confirm physical handover and returns.

Fairness and incidents

The platform uses transparent quotas and waiting lists to discourage monopolisation. Important access or incident decisions are subject to authorised human review. A damage report is not automatically a finding of responsibility or a financial penalty.

Institution-specific terms

Before a real launch, the responsible operator must adopt and publish its actual usage agreement, acceptable-use policy, cancellation conditions, privacy terms and complaints process. This page describes software principles, not a final legal agreement.
