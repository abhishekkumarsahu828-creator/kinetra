Privacy overview.

How the current software handles resource-sharing data.

Information recorded

Account details, institutional membership and role, training verification status, resource listings, booking purpose and times, condition reports, incident descriptions, operational notifications and audit events are stored to run the platform.

Who can access information?

A borrower can view their transactions. Authorised owners and institutional managers can access only the records relevant to the resources and organisations they supervise. Public resource descriptions may be visible without authentication. Uploaded private condition evidence is delivered through permission-checked routes.

Retention, deletion and hosting

Retention periods, account rights, organisational contact details, legal basis and support procedures must be defined by the institution before launch. This software does not itself guarantee statutory compliance or automatic deletion of all historic audit records.

Contact

Contact the institution administering your resource-sharing programme. A real deployment must publish an accurate controller/operator identity, privacy notice and support contact before accepting real personal information.
